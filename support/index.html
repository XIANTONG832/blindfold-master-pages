Technical support

Keep your next solve moving.

Find quick answers for scanning, memo practice, algorithms, and your account-free local setup.

Camera scanning

Give the app a well-lit, unobstructed view of one face at a time. Keep the stickers square to the guide and avoid glare. If recognition is not reliable, use manual color entry to correct the face before confirming.

Course setup

Blindfold Master uses the green-top, red-front course orientation with UF as the edge buffer and UFL as the corner buffer. Confirm your physical cube orientation matches the guide before creating a memo.

Algorithm practice

The catalog is available offline. Use the search and category controls to revisit edge, corner, parity, and orientation formulas. Practice mode lets you focus on recall without starting a full solve.

Data and device

Cube scans and practice history are stored locally on your device. The app does not require an account. For the best experience, use iOS 18 or later and keep enough free storage for exported reference images.
