## Description (under 4,000 characters)

Blindfold Master turns 3×3 blindfolded cubing into a focused, repeatable practice routine. Scan your cube, build a memo, study the exact algorithm you need, and rehearse the solve before the blindfold goes on.

### A calm workflow for serious practice

Blindfold solving is a chain of small decisions. Blindfold Master keeps the important ones visible: the cube state, the buffer, the memo path, and the next algorithm. Use it beside your timer, at the practice table, or anywhere you want a structured session.

### Scan your cube—or enter it yourself

Use the six-face camera workflow to recognize a physical cube one face at a time. Clear guides help you line up each face, and a review step lets you confirm the detected colors before continuing. Prefer complete control? Enter the cube colors manually or work from a scramble.

### Memo with a familiar course setup

Blindfold Master follows a clear course orientation: green on top, red in front, UF as the edge buffer, and UFL as the corner buffer. Keep the orientation visible while you create your memo and follow the path through the cube.

### Study the formula you need

The offline algorithm catalog includes 818 edge and corner algorithms, plus special formulas for parity and orientation. Search, browse, and revisit an algorithm without waiting for a connection. It is a practical reference for learning letter schemes, drilling difficult cases, and preparing for a full attempt.

### Three ways to practice

• Memo practice: focus on seeing and recalling the path.
• Algorithm practice: drill the formulas that slow you down.
• Full simulation: combine recognition, memo, and execution in one session.

Replay your path, return to a previous formula, and export a long reference image when you want a quick study sheet.

### Designed for your device

Your cube scans, memo sessions, settings, and practice history stay on your device. Blindfold Master does not require an account and is designed to work offline after installation. Camera access is used only when you choose the scanning workflow; manual entry remains available.

Whether you are learning your first blindfold method, building reliable recall, or refining a fast solve, Blindfold Master gives every attempt a clear next step.

Blindfold Master is an independent training tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the World Cube Association.

### Requirements

• iPhone or iPad
• iOS 18.0 or later
• Camera access is optional and used for cube scanning
