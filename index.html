A complete blindfold routine

From first scan to final execution.

Every screen has one job: make your next blindfolded solve easier to understand, repeat, and improve.

01

Scan or enter a scramble

Use the six-face camera workflow to recognize your cube, or enter colors manually when you want complete control. Review the detected state before you continue.

02

Memo with a clear structure

Work from the familiar UF edge buffer and UFL corner buffer. Keep your memo focused, with the course color scheme and piece labels visible when you need them.

03

Study the exact algorithm

Browse an offline catalog of 818 edge and corner algorithms, plus special parity and orientation formulas. No connection is needed during practice.

04

Rehearse before you commit

Choose memo practice, algorithm practice, or a complete simulation. Replay your path, revisit a formula, and export a long image for a quick reference sheet.

A calmer way to train

Turn “I hope I remember” into a routine.

Blindfold solving is a chain of small decisions. Blindfold Master keeps those decisions visible: the cube state, the buffer, the memo path, and the algorithm you need next.

Use it beside your timer, at the practice table, or on a flight with no signal. Your cube states and practice history stay on your device so the session remains yours.
